Our approach

A healthier, quieter, more comfortable home

Passive House construction delivers comfort that goes beyond square footage — cleaner air, consistent temperatures, and near-total quiet. Built for the people who matter most to you.

Healthier air

Continuous fresh-air ventilation with filtration keeps allergens and pollutants out — critical for aging parents or anyone with respiratory sensitivities.

Library-quiet rooms

Consistent comfort

No hot spots, cold drafts, or damp corners. The same comfortable temperature in every room, year-round, without running the heating system constantly.

The standard

What is Passive House?

Passive House (Passivhaus) is the world's most rigorous energy and comfort standard for buildings. It's not a style — it's a physics-based science that guarantees your home uses a fraction of the energy of a conventional build while maintaining a healthier, more comfortable indoor environment year-round. Every UnityHaus ADU is designed and built to meet this standard.

Super-insulation

Thick, continuous insulation on every surface — walls, roof, and floor slab — eliminates heat loss through the building shell. No shortcuts, no gaps.

Airtight envelope

A continuous air barrier wraps the entire home and is verified by a blower-door test. Random air leaks — the biggest source of energy waste — are eliminated.

Thermal bridge–free

Anywhere two materials meet — a window frame, a foundation edge, a wall corner — heat can sneak out. We engineer every junction to stop that path entirely.

Triple-glazed windows

Three panes of glass with thermally broken frames capture free solar heat in winter and reject it in summer — while staying frost-free on the inside.

Heat Recovery Ventilation

An HRV unit continuously exchanges stale indoor air for fresh outdoor air, recovering 80–90% of the heat in the process. Fresh air, no energy waste.
